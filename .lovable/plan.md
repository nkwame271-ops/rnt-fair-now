# Remove public government and ministry branding

## Scope
- Remove public references presenting the platform as part of the Government, Republic of Ghana, Ministry of Works & Housing, or Rent Control Department.
- Keep internal staff portals, operational records, notifications, and statutory workflows unchanged.

## Changes
- Update the public homepage header, descriptive sections, footer, and search/social metadata to use the independent `Rent Control Ghana` name.
- Remove government agency promotional wording and named ministry examples from the public homepage.
- Update public sign-in, password recovery, premium, developer, and verification pages where those affiliations are shown.
- Remove coats of arms and government/department headings from public verification views.
- Update public machine-readable descriptions (`llms.txt` and structured metadata) so they no longer claim official government status.

## Verification
- Search all public-facing source for the removed affiliations.
- Check desktop and mobile public pages visually.
- Confirm the current build completes without errors.
